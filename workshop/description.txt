[h1]DogShare - co-op dogs for guests[/h1]

In co-op the game only allows [b]one dog for the whole session[/b], and only the host can rescue it. DogShare lets the host hand dogs to the other players.

[list]
[*]The host can keep [b]3 dogs[/b] in co-op again, like single-player.
[*][b]F7[/b] (host): gives the dog closest to you to the player closest to you. Up to 3 per guest.
[*][b]F8[/b] (host): takes back the guest's dog closest to you.
[*]A guest's dog follows them and fights for them, is saved in the host's world, and links back to them when they rejoin.
[*][b]Guests use the dog like their own:[/b] the game's Commands / Pet / Feed prompt, the Teammates card and its orders menu, rename, skills, first aid, gear (collar, vest, saddlebag) and the saddlebag's storage. No extra keys.
[*]Petting and feeding animations are synced for everyone with DogShare (the base game only shows them to the player doing it).
[*]Guest dogs are kept fed and don't need healing from the guest.
[/list]

[b]Everyone should subscribe:[/b] the host needs it to give dogs, and guests need it for the prompts and Teammates card.

[h2]Requires[/h2]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834614258]ZED ZONE Code Mod Loader[/url] - run its install.cmd once, then start the game through Steam.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Host: rescue a dog and set it to follow you (a dog told to stay won't follow its new owner).
[*]Stand next to your friend and press F7.
[*]Rescue another one. You keep up to 3, each guest gets up to 3.
[*]F8 next to a guest's dog takes it back.
[/olist]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]If a guest leaves, their dogs wait in the host's world until they come back.
[/list]

Config: BepInEx/config/girthy_mods.zedzone.dogshare.cfg (keys, max dogs per guest, range, keep fed).
